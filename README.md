# "Munbt" Automated Vertical Farming & Precision Service Robotics
> **Funded AgriTech Startup (Startup Syria & Oxfam Grant, Oct 2026)**  
> **Industrial Mechanical Design, Additive Manufacturing & Service Robotics**

## 1. Project Background & Funding
"Munbt" is an automated, high-density vertical agricultural structure engineered to maximize crop yield per square meter while slashing irrigation water consumption by up to 85%. Developed as a scalable commercial prototype, this project secured competitive funding from **Startup Syria** backed internationally by **Oxfam (October 2026)**.

## 2. Engineering Contributions & Mechanical Systems

### 🌿 A. "Munbt" Automated Vertical Farming
- **Tubular Structural Base:** Engineered a 3-tier vertical agricultural rack elevated 60 cm above ground level on welded structural steel frames, verified via SolidWorks FEA for deflection under saturated substrate weight.
- **Closed-Loop Fluidics:** Integrated multi-tier drip manifold regulated by solenoid valves, soil moisture probes, and electrical conductivity (EC) sensors for precise micro-irrigation.

### 🤖 B. Service Robotics & "Manarah" Guidance Robot (Saudi Arabia)
- **Deployment Context:** Contributed to the mechanical manufacturing, assembly layout, and chassis design of the **"Manarah" (منارة)** smart guidance service robot, deployed to assist visitors and pilgrims in Makkah and Madinah, Saudi Arabia.
- **Industrial Prototyping:**
  - Designed internal structural mountings, electronic service trays, and ergonomic exterior panels for high-traffic public operation.
  - Optimized component fitment and modular wire harnessing for fast field maintenance.

### ⚙️ C. Advanced Additive Manufacturing (SWB AI)
- **Multi-Material 3D Printing:** Sliced and fabricated functional robotic enclosures using Bambu Studio and Bambu Lab AMS with carbon-fiber reinforced PETG and impact-resistant ABS.
- **Embedded NFC Hardware:** Executed automated mid-print pause routines to embed contactless **NFC tags** inside printed brackets for digital part identification and maintenance lifecycle tracking.
